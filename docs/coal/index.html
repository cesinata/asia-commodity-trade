Coal exports: Australia and Indonesia

This dashboard compares the coal exports of Australia and Indonesia: export volumes, average export values and destination markets.

Source: UN Comtrade.

Both countries sell most of their coal to buyers in Asia. The charts show where each country’s exports went in the latest year, and how much went to China over time.

Comparison of reported trade with China

Each exporter records the coal it ships to China, and China records the coal it receives from each country. The two figures can differ because shipments may leave in one year and arrive in the next, and because countries attribute trade to partner countries in different ways. Differences above 10% are marked “Review”.

Source: UN Comtrade. Difference = (China’s figure − the exporter’s figure) ÷ the exporter’s figure.

About the data

Source
UN Comtrade, the United Nations database of trade statistics reported by national customs authorities.
Products
Coal (HS 2701) and lignite (HS 2702), added together.
Period
Calendar years, as reported by each country.
Measures
Volume in tonnes. Value in US dollars at current prices, not adjusted for inflation. Average export value is total value divided by total tonnes.
Shares
Each destination’s tonnes divided by the total for all destinations in that year.
Limitations
The data does not separate coal used for power generation from coal used for steelmaking. The most recent year may be incomplete or revised. The charts describe what changed and do not explain why.
